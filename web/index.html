How this goes

1 Sign the Stellar setup Costs nothing. Nothing has happened yet if you stop here.
2 Burn the USDC on Base This is the step that commits your money.
3 We set up your Stellar account Done while Circle attests, so it costs you no extra wait.
4 USDC arrives Under a minute. Stellar does take Circle's fast transfers, whatever the documentation reads like.

Your transfers

Your keys stay yours. You make your own wallet in Freighter and hold its key. We never generate or hold one. What we pay for, when it is needed, is the account existing on the ledger.

The signature comes before the burn on purpose: there is no point at which your money is taken and cannot be delivered.

This charges for the interface and for the setup on the far side, not for access to the rail. You can always call CCTP yourself and pay nothing.
